About Deadlock

A New MOBA from Valve

Deadlock is an online MOBA in development from Valve, currently in invite-only playtesting.

The Setting

Deadlock takes place in the Cursed Apple, the in universe equivalent of New York City, with all gameplay being in the streets of the city. In Deadlock the supernatural is common and the public has accepted it's presence for about 50 years by the events of the game.

The Ritual

Two patrons, the Hidden King and the Archmother, are trying to enter this plane, promising to fulfill the wish of anyone who helps them complete the ritual. The ritual takes place on the streets of the Cursed Apple where two teams of six try to prevent each other from summoning their patron.

Gameplay Mechanics

Objectives

In Deadlock there are three lanes, each with three primary objectives. In order of encounter there is the Guardians, The Walkers, and the Watchers (or base guardians). Each of these objectives are stationary towers that have different attacks and health values. After the lane objectives there are two more primary objectives to complete, the shrines and the patron. The shrines are invincible until at least one watcher is completely destroyed. The patron, similarly, is invincible until both shrines are destroyed. Each objective, with the exception of the patrons, has bounties that the player who destroys the objective claims and can use to boost themselves.

Side Objectives

Side Objectives are not required to beat the Ritual, however they can be advantageous to the team who completes them . There are currently 3 side objectives in Deadlock; The Urn, The Unstable Rift, And The Midboss. Each side objective is different. The Urn is a solo objective, something you pick up and carry across the map to get permanent buffs and a bounty. The Unstable rift is a king of the hill style objective where it forms on one of the side lanes and creates an arena where a team must stay inside to capture the it. When the Rift is completed everyone on the team shares a bounty and it spawns a small amount of Rift Troopers that are twice as effective as normal Troopers and offer no bounty. Finally the Midboss is a large creature under Mid Lane that upon defeat will give permanent buffs and a bounty, additionally it also drops the Rejuvenator which is a crystal that breaks in three heavy melee attacks and provides the attacking team single use, on the spot, revives per heavy melee.

The Shop

The shop is where players can use their bounties to purchase items that modify their stats and abilities to gain an edge over the rival team. There are three categories of items; Spirit, Weapon, and Vitality. Each category tends to modify certain things such as weapon damage, damage types and base stats. Additionally there are two different types of items. General items and Imbue items, General items will affect stats across the board, where Imbue items tend to affect particular abilities. In addition the modifications that items provide can be passive or active, active items giving stats while active and passive items being passive.

Heroes

44 Different Heroes to Choose from

As of now there are 44 different heroes in deadlock, each with unique abilities and play-styles.

Types of heroes

Each hero has different base stats and weapons. Some heroes have a strong weapon, others have strong spirit (ability) power, and others specialize in vitality and absorbing damage. These different types of heroes create different archetypes such as Tank, Gun, Spirit, and Support heroes. Each character through the use of items can become any archetype but they tend to lean towards specific archetypes.
